Wharf WOD 2.0: a fresh look and faster setup. A new Wharf WOD logo and icon, and every workout has its own colour: For Time orange, EMOM pink, AMRAP blue, Tabata green. Set a timer by turning the stopwatch bezel for AMRAP and For Time, or scrolling the wheels for EMOM and Tabata, then hit the big START bar. Home draws each workout's shape so you see what you'll start, and on iPad your last workout leads. While you work, the clock is huge and steady, rest is pink, and the bar fills round by round. Pause, Finish and Stop live in one place at the bottom, and you can scroll the count to fix your AMRAP rounds. On Apple Watch: the same colours and timelines, every timer remembers your setup as you change it, and a new Beeps only voice.
