Apple Watch fix. The last update asked for your heart rate in a way that could switch Workouts off in Health. That stopped the heart rate showing, and the beeps when your wrist was down. Wharf WOD now asks for the heart rate alone, and if Workouts is off it tells you on Home and how to turn it back on (on the watch: Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Wharf WOD). Also new on phone and watch: the halfway pips have their own pitch and are as loud as the other beeps.
