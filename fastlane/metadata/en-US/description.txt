The no-fuss workout timer for functional fitness. Pick a format, set your time, hit start — then just work.

FOUR TIMERS, ZERO CLUTTER
• AMRAP — as many rounds as possible in your time cap
• FOR TIME — race the clock, counting up or down
• EMOM — every minute (or any interval) on the minute
• TABATA — work/rest intervals with clear phase changes

VOICE CUES THAT CARRY
Three recorded voice packs (or shuffle them) call the moments that matter: the countdown, GO, halfway, last round, ten seconds, and the final 5-4-3-2-1. Cues play even with the ringer on silent — like a coach who never loses count.

BUILT FOR THE GYM FLOOR
• Giant timer digits you can read from across the room
• The screen stays awake for your whole workout
• Portrait or landscape — prop it on a bench or a box
• Colour-coded phases: green means work, you know the rest
• Haptic hits on every round change

TIMING YOU CAN TRUST
The clock is wall-clock accurate. Lock your phone, switch apps mid-workout — your rounds and intervals stay exactly where they should be.
