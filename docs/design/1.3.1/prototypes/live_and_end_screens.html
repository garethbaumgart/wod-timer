Live and end screens, in each workout's colours

Each workout now wears its Home colour while it runs: For Time orange, EMOM pink, AMRAP blue, and Tabata green for work and pink for rest. The progress bar is the Home timeline, filling block by block. Every screen below is running: tap AMRAP to count a round, tap PAUSE to see the paused screen, hold STOP to end, and use the speed buttons to watch the blocks fill.

Things to decide:
