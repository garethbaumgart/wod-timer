Bezel, wheels and a full-width START

Your three picks put together: the stopwatch bezel for AMRAP and For Time, wheels side by side for EMOM and Tabata, and START as an edge-to-edge slab. Every screen works, so try them on your phone. Steps match the app: whole minutes on the bezel, 15 seconds for the EMOM interval, 5 seconds for Tabata work and rest.

Three things I added, each easy to drop:

1. For Time's count direction is an Up / Down switch under the bezel, replacing the small "tap to change" line.

2. EMOM and Tabata show the Home timeline under the wheels, redrawn as you change them.

3. Headings are white to match the new Home. Today they're orange.
